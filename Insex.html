An Open Letter

I've never been good with words out loud. So I wrote this instead.

Mother's Day, 2025

Dear Mom,

There are things I've wanted to say for years, but every time I tried, the words felt too small for the feeling. So let me try one more time.

Thank you — not just for the meals you cooked or the clothes you washed or the nights you stayed up when I was sick. Thank you for the invisible things. The way you believed in me before I believed in myself. The way you never made me feel like a burden, even when I was one.

I've watched you carry so much, so quietly. And it made me understand what real strength looks like. Not loud. Not dramatic. Just steady, warm, and endlessly patient.

I know I don't say it enough. I know I sometimes forget to call. I know I've taken your presence for granted in ways I'm only now starting to understand. But know this — every version of me that is good, is good because of you.

You are my home. My first friend. My loudest cheerleader. My safest place.

Happy Mother's Day, Mom. Today and every day.

With all my love,
Your Child ❤️
